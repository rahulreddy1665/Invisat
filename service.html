Our Products

At Invisat Pharmaceuticals, we manufacture a wide range of prescription pharmaceuticals and over-the-counter nutraceuticals.

Our product portfolio covers therapies in cardiology, diabetology, orthopedics, gastroenterology, nephrology, neurology, gynecology, general medicine, and more. We also produce health supplements including multivitamins, proteins, minerals, and antioxidants.

With state-of-the-art facilities utilizing advanced technology, we ensure the highest quality standards for purity, safety and efficacy. Our affordable, accessible medications and supplements are available through physicians, pharmacies and international distributors across South India and globally.
